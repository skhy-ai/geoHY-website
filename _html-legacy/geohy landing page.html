Exothermic Co-Generation

Serpentinization is a violently exothermic reaction. By routing the returning supercritical fluids through surface heat exchangers, we strip the thermal energy ($\Delta H$) before separation, driving Organic Rankine Cycle (ORC) turbines to power the entire site.

1. Downhole Exothermic Heat

High-volume rock hydration releases extreme thermal energy (>270°C) into the returning extraction fluid mixture.

2. Surface Heat Exchange

Fluids pass through the primary HX unit, stripping the heat into a closed-loop secondary working fluid before chemical separation.

3. ORC Turbines

Organic Rankine Cycle turbines convert the heat into zero-emission electricity to power AI robotics and compression systems.

Pioneering with AI & Robotics

The realization of the Lithospheric Reactor is contingent upon resolving substantial geochemical and engineering challenges. GeoHy utilizes an advanced AI technology stack to mitigate HPHT (High-Pressure, High-Temperature) risks and map expansive lattice structures.

AI Geophysical Inversion

To avoid the "Serpentinite Trap" (drilling into spent, oxidized rock), our neural networks process massive seismic and gravimetric datasets to identify pristine olivine.

  • Target Vp: > 8.0 km/s (Fresh Olivine)
  • Target Density: > 3.2 g/cm³
  • Avoid Vp: < 5.5 km/s (Altered)

Autonomous Downhole Robotics

Serpentinization creates a depleted "rind" acting as a severe diffusion barrier within the lattice. We deploy AI-driven robotics capable of operating at 270°C to ream the wellbore.

  • Action: Scheduled Mechanical Reaming
  • Effect: Removes 5-10cm depleted layer
  • Result: Resets ODE reaction kinetics to Year 0

Plasma Lattice Drilling

Conventional mechanical rotary bits exhibit unacceptable wear in ultra-hard ultramafic lithologies. We utilize next-generation contactless directed-energy plasma drilling to achieve extensive horizontal lattice expansion.

  • Constraint: Extreme HPHT environment
  • Material: High-nickel CRAs
  • Geometry: Intersecting Lattice Grid

The Golden Zone Refinery

By targeting depths of 4 to 5 kilometers, we reach extreme hydrostatic pressures (50-80 MPa). The injected $H_2O$ and $CO_2$ transition into a fiercely supercritical state with near-zero surface tension.

At 270°C, the natural Awaruite ($Ni_3Fe$) and Magnetite catalysts trigger Fischer-Tropsch Type (FTT) synthesis. We don't just extract gas; we manufacture high-density fuels.

Solid Carbon Mineralization

Supercritical $CO_2$ reacts with magnesium to form solid Magnesite ($MgCO_3$), ensuring verifiable, permanent carbon-negative operation.

Hy10 Baseload Gas

A synthetic blend of 10% Hydrogen and 90% Methane. Safely transportable via existing midstream pipelines, burning cleaner in standard gas turbines.

Abiotic Liquid Hydrocarbons

Secondary cracking produces stable Paraffins and energy-dense Aromatics (C4 to C12) providing the high-thrust energy requisite for aviation and heavy industry.
